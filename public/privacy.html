Official Privacy Policy & Google OAuth Disclosure

Privacy Policy — GraceBooks Church Finance

Effective Date: January 1, 2025  •  Last Updated: October 2026 (v2.4.0)  •  Architecture: Local-First & Zero-Knowledge

1. Overview & Local-First Privacy Commitment

GraceBooks Church Finance (“GraceBooks”, “we”, “our”, or “the Application”) is a free, open-source, local-first church fund accounting and financial stewardship web application designed for church treasurers, finance committees, and ministry boards.

We operate on a strict Zero-Knowledge, Local-First architecture. We do not operate centralized backend servers that collect, harvest, read, or monetize your congregation’s financial transactions, donor lists, or accounting records. All church ledger data remains strictly under your control in your local web browser and, only when explicitly enabled by you, in your personal or church Google Drive account.

2. Information We Access & Collect

  • Local Church Ledger Data (Stored on Your Device): Includes church profile settings, ministry fund balances, bank/petty cash accounts, collection records, donor/member names, expense vouchers, and annual budgets. This data is stored locally inside your browser’s localStorage and never leaves your device unless you export a backup or initiate a Google Drive sync.
  • Google Account & Drive Data (Optional Cloud Sync): If you choose to sign in with Google to back up or sync your church ledger across devices, the Application requests your basic profile information (name, email address, and profile photo) via Google Sign-In and restricted access to app-created files in your Google Drive.

3. Google User Data & Google Drive API Scope Disclosure

When you voluntarily connect your Google Account to use the Cloud Sync & Backup feature, GraceBooks Church Finance requests authorization for the following specific Google OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • openid, email, and profile: Used solely to display your signed-in account name, email address, and avatar inside the application header and sync modal so you know which Google account is connected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (Per-File Restricted Google Drive Access): Used exclusively to create, read, update, and list your church ledger backup files (such as gracebooks_church_finance_db.json and fiscal year archive files) inside your Google Drive.

Strict Scope Limitation Guarantee: Because GraceBooks Church Finance only requests the non-broad drive.file scope, the Application cannot see, list, read, modify, or delete any other personal or organizational files, photos, or documents stored in your Google Drive. It can only access the specific JSON ledger files created directly by GraceBooks Church Finance.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

GraceBooks Church Finance’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We do not transfer, sell, rent, or share your Google user data or church financial data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or marketing.
  • We do not use Google user data or your financial ledger files to train artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning models.
  • No human at GraceBooks Church Finance reads your Google Drive files or local ledger data unless you explicitly share a sanitized snippet when requesting technical support.

5. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

  • Direct Browser-to-Google HTTPS Transmission: When syncing with Google Drive, your browser communicates directly with Google’s official APIs (googleapis.com) over TLS/HTTPS encryption. Your ledger files do not pass through any intermediate third-party storage server.
  • Input Sanitization & Integrity Checks: Imported JSON backup files and CSV spreadsheets are validated and sanitized locally in your browser to guard against prototype pollution, malformed schemas, and spreadsheet formula injection (DDE payloads).

6. Data Retention, Access Revocation & Deletion Rights

Because you own 100% of your data, you can inspect, export, or permanently delete your data at any time without needing to contact us:

  • Deleting Local Browser Data: You can clear or reset your local ledger at any time from the Data Mode Settings modal inside the Application or by clearing your browser’s site data.
  • Deleting Google Drive Backups: Because your cloud backups are stored directly inside your own Google Drive account, you can view, move, or permanently delete gracebooks_church_finance_db.json at any time directly within Google Drive.
  • Revoking Google OAuth Permissions: You can sign out inside the Application’s Cloud Sync modal or revoke GraceBooks Church Finance’s access at any time via your Google Account Third-Party Access & Permissions page.
