2. Description of Service & Local-First Operation

GraceBooks Church Finance is a browser-based church fund accounting, tithe & offering collection tracker, expense voucher generator, and monthly board reporting application.

  • Client-Side Execution: All financial calculations, fund reconciliations, and report generations occur directly inside your web browser.
  • Optional Google Drive Sync: Users may optionally connect a Google Account to back up and synchronize their church ledger JSON files directly to their own Google Drive using the restricted drive.file scope.
